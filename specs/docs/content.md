## Content Integrity
The original source files contain the detailed grammar, vocabulary, speaking, writing,
listening, reading, duration, fee, eligibility and exam-preparation content.

When converting to JSON:
- preserve meaning and terminology;
- do not add unsupported promises;
- do not change prices/durations;
- keep lists as arrays rather than long prose strings.

## Content Not Yet Supplied
The source files do not provide enough factual content for:
- About-page biography/history
- instructors
- testimonials
- reviews
- social media
- email
- business hours
- detailed gallery items
- downloadable materials

Use empty/template content structures for these instead of fabricated content.
